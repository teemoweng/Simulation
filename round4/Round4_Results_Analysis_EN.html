WHAT WORKED

Three Decisions That Paid Off

✓TERRA launch was correctly timed — captured 47.6% Vodite value share in 1 period

Snipers entered Vodites in P3 with SESENEW. We followed in P4 with TERRA. Result: 48% / 48% split, locking out Marvels (2%) and Rocket (3%) — possibly permanently.

Why: Rogers' diffusion model — Innovators + early Adopters are first-mover advantaged, but a near-immediate follower can still split the segment. The $2.6M R&D + $1.6M launch ads + low-volume penalty cost (~$3M) all paid back in one period.
✓POTONECRP3 switch on TONE cut unit cost from $77 to $56 (−27%)

TONE CAM doubled from $6.5M to $13.2M. Combined with Savers segment growth of +19.6%, TONE became a reliable cash engine. Savers share 31.3% is industry-best.

Why: One $100K R&D cost-reduction project, applied to a high-volume product (191K units), generates ~$5–6M of incremental CAM. R&D ROI well over 50x.
✓TOHO turned profitable: $4.5M swing from −$1.95M → +$2.57M

Production rose from 60K to 105K. Experience curve dropped unit cost from $124 to $100. Shoppers share now 27.9% (highest in market).

Why: Three lost-sale rounds finally ended once we built enough inventory. Even at this low awareness (19%), TOHO converts when stocked — a strong product-market fit signal.
WHAT WE LEFT ON THE TABLE

Three Decisions That Underperformed Their Potential

1. Sonites R&D spend = $0

The decision guide called for two projects: POTOPS cost-reduction (~$250K, would cut TOPS unit cost from $113 → ~$100, saving ~$2M/yr) and a TOHO upgrade (~$500K, addressing the growing Shoppers ideal-point on Power and Display). Neither was funded. Meanwhile Rocket invested $1.2M in R&D. Marvels' MOVE has already been upgraded to Power 84 — TOPS will fall behind on perceived power within 1–2 periods.

2. TOHO produced 100K vs guide's 120K — capped its own profit

TOHO sold out all 105K units it produced (using 5K of buffer above the 100K plan from the experience-curve buffer). The decision guide recommended 120K. Shoppers segment is growing +35.2% next period — every unit of unmet TOHO demand is a lost share point that becomes harder to win back. Estimated lost CAM: 15K units × ~$25/unit = ~$0.4M, plus permanent market-share decay.

3. Ad spending levelled at $1.31M per Sonites brand instead of doubling down on TOPS

The advertising experiment showed TOPS +20% ad spend would yield +$447K CAM (vs TOHO +$94K, TONE +$73K). The guide recommended TOPS ad of $1.7M, TONE $1.5M, TOHO $1.8M. Actual: all three at $1.31M. Net effect: we under-spent on the highest-ROI channel by ~$390K, while over-spending on a channel that already had product-market fit but needed production not ads (TOHO). Roughly $0.8M in missed CAM.

SIGNALS FOR ROUND 5

What This Data Tells Us About Next Round's Plan

  1. Re-fund Sonites R&D — this is the most expensive miss. Two projects to revisit: POTOPS cost reduction (~$250K, ~$2M annual return) and a TOHO upgrade (~$500K, addresses Shoppers' ideal-point drift). Without these, P5–P6 will see regulatory share loss to Marvels/Rocket on regular cost-position basis.
  2. Shift advertising allocation toward TOPS. Experiment shows TOPS +20% ad = +$447K CAM (ROI 1.7x). Move at least $300–400K from TOHO ad (already saturated, needs production not ads) to TOPS. MOVE/SOLO outspend us 3x in Professionals — we must close.
  3. TOHO production target: 160–180K. Shoppers growing 35% next period; TOHO Shoppers share 27.9% (highest in industry). Build inventory now while awareness is rising. Lost sales here are lost share that compounds.
  4. TONE production: 230K+. P4 hit the ±20% production cap (191K vs 220K plan). With Savers +19.6%, demand will be 220–240K. Set plan at 230K so cap permits up to 276K if needed.
  5. TERRA: build on the lead. Add to advertising (was $1.6M; benchmark SESENEW at higher). Production 100K+ to ride the Vodite market growth (Vodites went from 0 → $106M industry retail in 2 periods).
  6. Buy Commercial Team Experiment ($41.75K). We have not validated whether to grow sales force beyond 145 FTE. Need data before committing further hires at $24K/FTE.
  7. Buy MDS (Multidimensional Scaling) for both markets ($83.5K). Existing semantic-scales data only gives 1D maps; MDS will show whether TOPS positioning needs to shift on Power dimension to match Pros' ideal-point drift.
