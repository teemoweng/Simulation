THE TWO HEADLINE MOVES

What Defined the Final Round

① R&D stayed at $0

No project funded for the second straight period. The planned POTOPS cost-reduction was never run — TOPS ended at a $193 base cost against Snipers' identically-specced SOLO at $174. The two upgrade projects (POTOPSV2, POSHOPV2) were never started; PEINNOV stayed on the shelf.

② Prices cut across all five brands

TONE −$20, TOPS −$19, TOHO −$25, TERRA −$30, TECHNO −$10. Partly to clear ~95K units of unsold Vodite inventory carried in from P5, partly to defend volume in a crowding low end. It moved units but surrendered the premium margins that had been our edge.
