PART 03 · WHAT WORKED — AND WHY

The 5 Decisions That Delivered +44% EBT

✓TONE became the industry's #1 brand by volume (162K)
Beat MOST (150K, Marvels' star), captured 32.6% share in Savers (vs 26.1% MOST).
Why it worked: Multi-layered strategy — ① price cut to $235 (vs MOST's $225 was no longer the cheapest), ② 60% advertising allocation to Savers (industry-highest in absolute dollars at $660K), ③ Mass Merch sales force of 28 (matching Marvels' 30), ④ Mass Merch coverage hit 48% (highest among all low-end brands). Marvels' MOST has 74 FTE on a single brand but only 38 in Mass — we beat them in the channel they should have owned.
✓TOPS at $500 captured Explorers crown + held HE silver
Volume +34% (98K → 131K), value share +1pp, contribution +48%.
Why it worked: Dropped TOPS from $520 to $500 to directly attack ROLL ($510). Result: ROLL volume −13%, TOPS volume +33%. Took #1 in Explorers (18.1% intent vs MOVE 16.8%). The price cut was margin-accretive too — unit cost dropped $143 → $127 via experience curve, so per-unit margin actually grew $188 → $182 despite the lower price. Volume × margin = $20.3M contribution.
✓POSHOP R&D completed on budget — Shoppers play ready
Specs delivered: Features 7 / Design 8 / Battery 48 / Display 23 / Power 51. Base cost $110.
Why it matters: POSHOP sits in the long-empty middle of the market — closer to Shoppers' ideal point (8.5, 1.6, 2.8) than any existing brand. Round 3 will launch a new brand on this platform, attacking a segment growing +28.7% next period and projected to be 30.6% of the market in 5 periods. No competitor has positioned here.
✓Salesforce expansion (60 → 110) paid off massively
Commercial costs +$1.1M produced +$10.6M increased contribution.
Why it worked: Period 1 experiment data predicted TOPS +30 FTE = +$1.7M; we added 25 to TOPS and got proportional impact. Distribution coverage rose: TONE Mass 44%→48%, TOPS Specialty 43%→45%, all channels Online +4pp. Sales-force ROI per FTE = ~$210K of incremental contribution.
✓Cut advertising 26% without losing share
Advertising dropped from $5.3M to $3.9M; share went UP on both metrics.
Why it worked: Period 1's experiment confirmed advertising was saturated. We cut research from 22-27% down to ~8% — saving $1.0M alone. The total $1.4M saved went into salesforce + R&D. Awareness still grew (TONE +1pp, TOPS +2pp average) because the spend that remained was concentrated in target segments.
PART 04 · WHAT WENT WRONG — AND WHY

4 Issues Round 3 Must Address

!Issue 1: TONE production crisis (2nd round in a row)
Planned 135K, demand exceeded the auto-adjust ceiling of 162K. Lost an estimated $2–4M in contribution.
Root cause: Underestimated demand acceleration. We knew P1 sold 118K with a 110K plan; we should have realized that with market growth +16.5% AND a price cut AND a stronger ad mix, demand could easily exceed 180K. We were anchored on the production-plan equals-sold-volume mental model — but the auto-adjust ceiling kept biting us.

Round 3 fix: Plan TONE at 200K minimum (probably 220K to be safe). The ±20% range would be 176–264K, accommodating any reasonable demand surprise.
!Issue 2: Lost Professionals leadership to Snipers' SOLO
SOLO dropped price $510→$499, lifted advertising massively, and took #1 in Pros (29.0% vs TOPS 25.6%).
Root cause: We dropped TOPS to $500 to attack ROLL — but Snipers attacked TOPS at the same time with $499. Without a TOPS R&D upgrade (POTOPS2 was deferred), we couldn't differentiate on product, only on price. Snipers won the price race by $1.

Round 3 fix: ① Launch POTOPS2 R&D to upgrade TOPS toward Pros' moving ideal (Power 5.7 ideal vs current perceived 5.5, Battery 4.9 ideal vs current 2.5). ② Consider taking TOPS back to $510 — competing on product, not price.
!Issue 3: Perceptual Objectives produced minimal movement
Set TONE targets Design 3.8 and Power 2.4 — both perceptions actually moved 0.0 from 1.5. TOPS Battery target 3.9 only moved 2.2 → 2.5.
Root cause: Advertising research budget was only 8.3-8.7% of total — the handbook recommends 10-15% for any meaningful repositioning. Also, ALL 8 brands have identical physical specs, so any perceptual repositioning is fighting against ground truth. Without R&D upgrade, advertising alone cannot move perception far.

Round 3 fix: Either commit to repositioning (15% research budget + Perceptual Objectives), or abandon it and use R&D-driven physical changes. Mixed signals don't work.
!Issue 4: R&D under-investment — we're falling behind
Our R&D spend: $1.4M. Rocket: $8.7M. Snipers: $9.2M. Marvels: $4.6M.
Why this is alarming: Rocket and Snipers are funding 6–7× more R&D than us. They're likely working on multiple Sonite upgrades AND/OR Vodite prototypes. The pipeline they're building will start shipping in Round 3–4. If we don't ramp R&D now, we'll face superior products from 2 of 3 competitors mid-game.

Round 3 fix: Launch 2–3 R&D projects: POTOPS2 (TOPS upgrade), POTONE2 (TONE deeper into Savers ideal + cost reduction), and consider a Vodite Feasibility Study to keep options open.
