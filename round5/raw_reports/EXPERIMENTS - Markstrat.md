## Advertising Experiment – Sonites Market – Period 4

This experiment is conducted by increasing advertising budgets in a selected regional market. The results of the study are used to project the level of awareness and the market share that would have been achieved nationwide by each brand with the same increase in advertising, and if competitive actions have remained unchanged. The impact on brand contribution is provided as well.

The results below would have been achieved by a given brand if its advertising budget had been increased by 20% and if competitive actions had remained unchanged.
