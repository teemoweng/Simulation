WHAT BROKE · ROOT CAUSES

Three Self-Inflicted Misses

1. R&D spend = $0 (the plan called for $2.8M)

None of the three planned projects — POTOPS cost-reduction, POTOPSV2 (Power 85), POSHOPV2 — were funded. The cost-reduction alone was forecast to return ~$2.7M/yr at a $300K cost (payback under two months). With no project, TOPS' base cost stayed at $179 while Snipers' identically-specced SOLO fell to $160 — a structural margin gap we handed them for free. No product upgrade landed either, so TOPS and TOHO stood still while ideal points kept drifting.

2. Both Vodite brands were massively over-produced

TECHNO: made 72K, sold 25K. TERRA: made 80K, sold 32K. Combined, ~95K units sat unsold, generating $1.9M of inventory-holding cost — the highest of any firm this period. The "plan at the upper edge of demand" heuristic, correct in a fast-growing duopoly, was wrong once Vodites became a five-brand fight and our share halved. We planned for the old market, not the new one.

3. Costs surged while revenue stalled → EBT fell

Revenue was flat (+0.8%), but commercial-team cost nearly doubled (~$4.1M → $7.7M), advertising rose (~$4.9M → $8.4M media), and inventory added $1.9M. The result: EBT dropped 7.8% in a period we had forecast a record. We spent like a firm pulling away, but the spending didn't convert to contribution because the product and production decisions underneath it were off.

!The duopoly thesis is breaking

At P4 we read Vodites as a Titans–Snipers lock-out (96% combined). One period later our share halved to 28% while Marvels' MEME revived to 9%. The late entrants were not permanently locked out — and splitting our own franchise across two premium-priced brands accelerated the loss.

SIGNALS FOR ROUND 6 (FINAL)

What This Round Demanded of the Last One

  1. Fund the POTOPS cost-reduction — finally. Two periods of a $19/unit gap vs SOLO is ~$5.7M of forgone contribution. Even in the last period it pays back.
  2. Stop over-producing Vodites; clear the glut. ~95K units of TERRA+TECHNO inventory must be sold down before building more. Cut Vodite production hard in P6.
  3. Pick ONE Vodite brand to defend. Two premium $710 brands cannibalise; consolidate spend behind the stronger one rather than funding both at sub-scale.
  4. Hold price discipline. The temptation in P6 will be to cut prices to move the inventory — but our premium positioning is the edge. (Spoiler: in P6 we cut anyway.)
  5. Re-allocate ads to the proven ROI brand (TOPS), not spread evenly. The same lesson as Round 4, still unlearned.
