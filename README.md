## Overview

This repository contains our group's project materials for the **Corporate Strategy and Economy Simulation** course at Imperial College Business School.

The simulation is based on **Markstrat**, a strategic marketing simulation platform where teams compete in a dynamic market environment, making decisions across product development, pricing, sales force allocation, and marketing investment.

Each round's results and decisions are documented as interactive HTML reports.
