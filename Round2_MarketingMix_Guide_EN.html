⚡ Round 2 Marketing Mix — core thesis

"Scale up production, fine-tune prices, slash advertising research." Experiment data clearly shows advertising is saturated (+20% loses $60–130K), while both brands sold out and lost units in Period 1. So this round: ① Aggressively raise production to capture growth; ② TONE drops slightly to defend against Marvels, TOPS raises further to keep capturing high-end; ③ Cut ad research from 22–27% to ~8% (awareness-maintenance level), saving ~$1.4M to fund teammate's R&D launches and salesforce expansion.
